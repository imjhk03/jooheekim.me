Sep 14–20, 2026

I had to take care of paperwork this week, so I couldn't focus much on development. I also went to the hospital and scheduled my surgery. I'll have the metal pins removed at the end of September, after the Chuseok holiday. I won't need to stay in the hospital long this time; I'll be discharged the day after surgery. I hope I can get it over with soon.

The app continues to sell in more than 10 countries, and its average daily sales are gradually increasing. It feels like the app is starting to find its footing. I used to worry every day about how to improve its performance and keep an eye on trends. Now it's doing reasonably well, so I worry less. I feel a little more at ease.

Of course, I won't settle into this sense of stability. There's still more I can do. New possibilities are beginning to come into view, one by one. I think I can use this app as a springboard to build new apps, too. I'll take things one at a time.

My week…
