Oct. 26–Nov. 1, 2026

While preparing the app for iPhone Duo, I thought about how it should look in landscape mode. Since it’s a widget app, there’s only so much I can do in the main app. I didn’t want to turn it into another calendar app, either. Then I remembered seeing two small widgets side by side in StandBy and thought I could use a similar layout: a calendar on the left and the Today list I’d already been showing on the right.

Apple announced a new iPad mini this week. I hadn’t been interested in the mini before, but the iPhone Duo got me thinking about the appeal of a larger screen. I wasn’t ready to give up my iPhone Pro for an iPhone Duo, though. Then I came across the idea of pairing an iPhone with an iPad mini and it caught my interest. I was disappointed that the announcement didn’t mention ProMotion or a 120 Hz refresh rate. I’d probably buy one if it had a 120 Hz display. I’ve been reading more lately and the iPad mini seems like it would make a good reading device. I’m now considering replacing my iPad Pro with one.

The swelling and tightness in my left arm are starting to improve with cold packs and gentle massage. The muscles are still quite tight, though, so I need to keep taking care of my arm. I have plans every weekend, so I haven’t been able to rest properly lately. I’ve been worried about not getting enough rest and haven’t been able to relax for days. Once I’m through next weekend’s plans, I’d like to set aside some time to rest.
