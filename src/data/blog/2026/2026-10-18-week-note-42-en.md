This week, I worked on some freelance projects. Tibo (@thsottiaux) has been announcing a new feature or improvement—or resetting usage limits—every day for [28 days](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2106845241357824205?a=z2bzaHnQ-W0BCkoO1QM&s=20), so I worked a little harder than usual to make the most of the tokens I was given. While strengthening the tests, I also cleaned up the related files, and it took longer than I expected. Still, it feels good to have tackled something I needed to do.

I also renewed my Apple Developer Program membership. My app revenue is now enough to cover the membership fee. I still have a long way to go, so I’m going to keep pushing toward my next goal.

After wrapping up a big project, I finally watched it—and it was so good. Every episode ended on such a compelling cliffhanger that I ended up staying up all night, watching until dawn.
