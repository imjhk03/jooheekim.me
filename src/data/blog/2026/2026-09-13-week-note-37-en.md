Sep 7–13, 2026

Several new things came up all at once this week, and I felt overwhelmed. A few tasks I needed to finish came up unexpectedly, and because they had firm deadlines, the week was a little difficult and busy.

The Apple event is a sign that the release of the new OS versions is just around the corner. Since the official releases are scheduled for September 14, I’m wrapping up development accordingly.

I’ve been gradually organizing and systematizing my Figma design files. Before, I used to make images in a hurry and use them as needed, but now I’ve decided to build a bit more structure. I’m preparing in advance because I think connecting Figma MCP later will make localization easier. I started by organizing them into broad categories: image files, App Store screenshots, and marketing images, and then further organizing the screenshots by version and language.

I also received some email and review feedback for the first time in a while and took some time to organize it. I was a little worried because I hadn’t received reviews or email feedback for some time, but after releasing the latest version and returning to ASO work, I’m starting to see some response. Once again, sales have started coming in from a variety of countries. I was reminded once more how important localization is.

I spent time with my family whenever I could and also made time for myself. I’m going to the hospital soon for my final pre-surgery examination and to schedule the surgery, so I’ve been mentally preparing myself.
